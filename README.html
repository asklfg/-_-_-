General Appearance

Assess the patient's age, sex, apparent distress, skin color, and overall appearance.

Document responsiveness, posture, and any obvious signs of distress or illness.

Trauma

Evaluate for mechanism of injury, areas of pain, deformity, and signs of bleeding.

Check for spinal tenderness, instability, and any life-threatening hemorrhage.

Respiratory

Assess respiratory rate, rhythm, depth, work of breathing, and breath sounds.

Check oxygenation, airway patency, and use of accessory muscles.

Cardiac

Evaluate pulse rate and quality, skin perfusion, capillary refill, and blood pressure.

Look for chest pain, dysrhythmia, hypotension, and signs of shock.

Refusal

Confirm the patient is alert and oriented, understands the risks, and is refusing care voluntarily.

Document mental status, capacity, alternatives offered, and refusal against medical advice.
